# Books: Harry Potter series

Have you ever wondered how it would be to have magic, but you cannot control it? 
Harry Potter, a 10-year-old boy adopted by the Dursleys, has that problem at the beginng of the book "Harry Potter and the philophoser's stone".
As (nearly) any kid at his age, he tries to fit in with the others and have fun, which isn't easy considering the uncontrollable magic part.

I liked the series because, however hard you try, 
no one could predict the end while not having read the book or seen any spoilers, which no one would do because it takes away the fun part of book reading.
The book series is also nice because, now and then, they have some good jokes in them, although not that often that the book strays from the plot.

Now, I thought it may be nice to have a small activity at the end of my articles, so imagine that you are a witch or wizard sitting in the train to Hogwarts.
A lady always goes through the train with a small trolley containing heaps of sweets. If you could create a sweet on that trolley,  what would it look like/smell/taste?
